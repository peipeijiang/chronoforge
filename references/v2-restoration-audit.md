# ChronoForge v2 restoration audit

This is the **skill v2**, reconstructed from the original cat-coffee production through **video V3**. Those version numbers are different. This update did not generate new paid media.

## What was reviewed

The local source overview contains 23 frames; all were inspected in this audit. Historical dense-window outputs, reference plans, Image2/Omni requests, L1/L2/L3 records, the original adapter and assembly script were also used as provenance. Historical QA findings are cited as historical findings, not new rewatch certification. The source's original audio was not independently re-audited here.

The public case excludes actual media, private URLs, task IDs, account data, approval records and local absolute paths. The illustrative requests are not ready or authorized until a new run supplies evidence and locks actual assets.

## The story, not merely coffee-making

The opening juxtaposes a serious orange-white coffee worker with a tabby's litter-box activity. A tent-origin odor, nose reaction and mask make the unpleasant implication legible. Ordinary bean preparation continues while the litter bag/scoop and later green deodorizer carry the cleanup subplot. Espresso, the cats' interaction, coffee/milk pours, a heart/garnish and an oversized floral mug provide process pleasure and the final visual payoff.

The visual joke suggests a “cat-coffee” association through editing. It does **not** establish literal feces entering a grinder or drink. The reviewed source shows a squat/litter context, not an explicit biological production sequence. Preserve the setup and implication without inventing unsupported physical continuity.

Direct source observations include a human offering a mask, the tabby carrying a litter tray, the black bag and pink-handled scoop, a green spray can, and the blue-rimmed floral mug. The historical generated script removed the human and assigned mask-wearing to the cat, simplified the tray movement, and additionally requested straw/tasting in the final pose. These are adaptations or uncertain fidelity details, not exact observed-source facts. A new run must resolve its permitted adaptation boundary rather than inherit these choices.

## Acceptance limits and remaining responsibilities

Historical video V3 was a delivery candidate **with disclosed warnings**, not a perfect clone. Its QA reported small brown litter clumps more explicit than the locked reference, incidental signage/decorative text, and other minor visual drift. The earlier V2 master was explicitly superseded for narrative failure.

The new helper scripts validate files, hashes, ranges, references and supplied review records. They do not understand the story, verify that a human truly approved, authenticate a reconciliation claim, enforce an account-wide budget or detect undeclared dependencies. The agent must perform those judgments honestly.

The restored skill keeps specialized roles clear: Watch is the evidence-analysis dependency; Image2 and Omni are provider models; FFmpeg performs local media operations. Earlier drama-skills/LuxReal and UGC/viral-pipeline research influenced creative or runtime design, but they are not all invoked during every run and their presence is not evidence that a video has passed QA.
