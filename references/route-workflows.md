# Route-specific evidence and creative work

## Recreation

Uploaded/local video → watch full-source evidence → attraction analysis → source story/state tracks → approved adaptation → editorial timeline. Record media parameters, exact extraction timestamps, observed cuts, visual observations, transcripts, audio actually heard and uncertainty. Inspect dense causal windows. Keep original timecodes if final timing changes.

analysis/source-evidence.json requires coverage.full_duration_reviewed=true, nonempty observations, and audio_review with findings or an unavailable limitation. Declare coverage only after actual inspection.

## Product original

Read product-ugc-pipeline for acquisition/cognition only; do not modify it or invoke its end-to-end video runner. Read applicable taxonomy/universal/trait/state-change references. Its defaults do not override Chronoforge TT/Omni models, built-in vision, Codex authoring, lock or serial paid runtime.

1. Confirm the page is the intended offer. Session/region-dependent pages use ego-browser. Extract every main/SKU/detail/use/packaging/poster asset plus exposed product-video evidence. No watch invocation.
2. Inspect every candidate with built-in vision. Copy portable evidence to evidence/product/: product_manifest.json, image_analysis.json, product_brief.json, category/risk/usage records and images/. Copied manifest/analysis local_path values are product-directory-relative and exactly cover the same set. Preserve original evidence and source URLs.
3. Create analysis/claim-ledger.json with claims: [{id, wording, status, evidence, approved_wording?}]. Status: supported, seller_claim, unresolved, rejected. Listing claims are not independent test results. Conflicting area/accessories/UV values remain unresolved. Seller claims require reviewed wording. AI imagery cannot substantiate measured performance.
4. Separate product facts, inferred buyer motivations and imagined settings. Optional research uses available agent-reach search/readers and ego-browser for session pages. Record URLs/dates/market relevance/limits. Subtitles and metadata cannot prove visual cuts; inspect playable imagery or mark those fields unknown. No mandatory scraping, invented reviews or trend rankings.
5. Codex creates the whole requested film: audience/context, selected claims, hook, benefit ladder, progression/proof/closing, visual rhythm and audio. Read creative history; tailor matrix choices to the product. Do not inherit irrelevant bedroom/late-night templates.
6. Write story-truth.json for the WHOLE duration. ad_variant_id identifies one film; C01/C02 are containers in it. Supporting beats use kind=detail|establishing|montage, supports=[beat ID], editorial_purpose. Fixed props may use one state plus configuration_lock=true. Narrative actions retain causal links.
7. Pin one SKU per ad; map beats to claim_ids. Establish permitted motion and unsafe omissions before references. Identity/operation grids are product-specific; actual scene/storyboard references follow. Do not force a state transition just to make endpoints different.

Product cognition also records state_change_contract and video_feasibility_plan with protect_product_configuration. Unknown mechanisms use omit_transition or hard_cut_only. These are verified facts/routing decisions, not invented values to satisfy a validator.

## Hybrid

Perform both evidence paths. Write adaptation_map in the story: source beat → target beat → preserved rhythm/composition → replaced product/action → reason/evidence. Product sources determine SKU/structure/function; source video supplies selected storytelling. Rewrite unsupported source actions explicitly. Source composition references must not override target product identity.

## Timeline v3

Use mode, editorial_duration, provider_clip_seconds, editorial_shots and containers. Shots and containers use editorial_range; shot_segments use shot_id and editorial_range, covering each shot once. Source-backed shots additionally retain measured source_range; added shots use adaptation_reason. Product-only originals omit source_range.

Containers keep provider_duration, retain_duration, completion_deadline. Execution jobs must match timeline IDs/durations. Three 10-second jobs are a possible 30-second allocation, not universal sufficient capacity. Keep actions complete before trim.

## Gates

Pre-image review checks full evidence, claim conflicts, plausible motion, whole story, time allocation and references. Missing optional research does not block; missing product/source evidence does. L1/human lock follow generated images; paid video requires current compiled plan and model review. Structural checks never certify meaning.
